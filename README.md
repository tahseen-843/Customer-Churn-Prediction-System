🚀 AI-Powered Customer Churn Prediction & Retention System
📌 Project Overview

Built an end-to-end Customer Churn Prediction System using Databricks, PySpark, SQL, Machine Learning, and Power BI.

The system analyzes customer behavior, predicts which customers are likely to leave the company, categorizes them into risk groups, identifies major churn drivers, and provides retention recommendations to improve customer loyalty.

🎯 Business Objective

Telecom companies lose revenue when customers discontinue services and switch to competitors.

This project helps businesses:

✅ Identify customers likely to churn

✅ Understand why customers leave

✅ Classify customers into risk categories

✅ Generate retention recommendations

✅ Support proactive customer retention strategies

🔍 Key Business Insights
Customer Churn Drivers

✅ Month-to-Month contract customers churn the most

✅ Customers with lower tenure are more likely to leave

✅ Higher monthly charges increase churn probability

✅ Customers without technical support show higher churn rates

✅ Certain payment methods contribute to increased churn risk

📋 Business Recommendations
Customer Retention Strategies

✅ Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts

✅ Provide promotional discounts for high-risk customers

✅ Offer premium technical support services

✅ Reward loyal customers through retention programs

✅ Proactively engage customers with high churn probability
